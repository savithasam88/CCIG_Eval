% C1 Existential Object, with a negated literal: there exist at least an object with property P1=V1 and property P2=V2 and property P3!=V3 and property P4=V4.
% POINT: this is a complex constraint to understand but an easy one to pass!
:- #count {X: object(X), hasProperty(X, P1', V1'), hasProperty(X, P2', V2'), not hasProperty(X, P3', V3'), not hasProperty(X, P4', V4'), P1'!=P2', P1'!=P3, P1'!=P4', P2'!=P3', P2'!=P4', P3'!=P4'} = 0.
