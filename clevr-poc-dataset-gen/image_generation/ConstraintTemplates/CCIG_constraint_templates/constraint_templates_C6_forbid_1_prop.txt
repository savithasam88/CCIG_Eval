% C6 Existential-Universal: At least one candidate object (P1=V1) must relate by D1 to every target object (P3!=V3).
c6_forbid_1_prop_counterexample(X) :- hasProperty(X, P1, V1), not hasProperty(Y, P3, V3), not hasRelationship(X, Y, D1).
c6_forbid_1_prop_satisfied :- hasProperty(X, P1, V1), not c6_forbid_1_prop_counterexample(X).
:- not c6_forbid_1_prop_satisfied.
